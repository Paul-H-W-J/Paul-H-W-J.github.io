YT-CLI

A read-only YouTube client for the terminal.

YT-CLI is a terminal app that shows the YouTube subscriptions of the person who signs in. It asks Google for one permission, https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.readonly, and it reads data only: it lists the channels you subscribe to, and it runs the searches you type. It cannot upload, comment, like, subscribe, unsubscribe, edit a playlist, or change any setting on your account.

What it does

YT-CLI reads your subscription list from the YouTube Data API, then fetches each channel's long-form uploads from YouTube's public RSS feeds and merges them into one grid, newest first. Arrow keys choose a video. Enter opens it in its normal YouTube watch page.

There is no home page, no recommendation feed, no Shorts, no trending page, no comments, no likes, and no autoplay.

YT-CLI login        # approve read-only access
YT-CLI              # your subscriptions
YT-CLI search       # find one specific video

Your data

The privacy policy says what YT-CLI accesses, how it uses it, where it is stored, and who it is shared with. You can revoke YT-CLI's access at any time from your Google account permissions page, or by running YT-CLI logout --forget-account.

Source

YT-CLI is open source. The downloadable command is one self-contained file with no dependencies outside the Python standard library, and the sign-in service is in the same repository.
